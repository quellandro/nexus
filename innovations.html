01 / ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Intelligence that
amplifies yours.

AI can help identify patterns, organize information, and draft new ideas. The best starting point is a clear problem and a person who can evaluate the result.

  • Creative tools
  • Pattern recognition
  • Human oversight
Explore a practical idea +

Design a study planner that groups tasks by subject. First build a clear interface with sample data; then consider where automated suggestions would help. Always let the learner review and edit a suggestion.

02 / CONNECTED SYSTEMS

Everything connects.
Make it meaningful.

Connected devices turn everyday observations into useful information. Good systems make their status clear and protect the people whose data they handle.

  • Smart environments
  • Useful data
  • Privacy by design
Explore a practical idea +

Prototype a room-comfort dashboard showing sample temperature, light, and humidity readings. Include timestamps and an offline indicator so users can distinguish current readings from old ones.

03 / DIGITAL EXPERIENCES

Complex technology.
Simple experiences.

A useful interface works across devices, respects different abilities, and helps people finish a task without unnecessary effort. Small design decisions make a lasting difference.

  • Accessible interfaces
  • Responsive design
  • Progressive web apps
Explore a practical idea +

Turn a responsive website into a PWA. Add a manifest and icons, introduce an offline fallback, and test installation on a real device. Keep navigation clear when browser controls are hidden.
